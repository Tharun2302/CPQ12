# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Changed
- **Exhibit Manager: the Agreement template list now starts on "Multi-Combination"** on the upload and edit forms, replacing "Not attached to an agreement template". Multi-Combination is the choice for every Migrate exhibit (Overage has none): the pair folder stays required and the exhibit is saved under its pair, never under the literal `multi-combination` tag, so existing exhibits and agreements are unaffected. Manage exhibits still pick Data-Sprawl or mange+sprawl.

### Fixed
- **Approval email links now say when the requester cancelled or deleted the approval.** Clicking the Team Lead, Technical or Legal link after the requester used "Cancel Approval" said "This document was denied.", as if an approver had rejected it. It now says "This approval was cancelled by the requester." (also the server's 409 message if Approve is clicked on an old page). After the requester deleted the approval, the link opened an empty dashboard with no message. It now shows "This approval request was deleted by the requester." and stops retrying. A cancel is recognised as a denied workflow with no denied step, since approvers always deny through their own step; no data is changed (`src/utils/approvalLinkNotice.ts`, `approval-step-guard.cjs`, the three team dashboards, `tests/unit/approvalStaleAction.test.tsx`).
- **Discount: clearing or correcting the Discount (%) box now really removes the old discount.** On the Configure page, deleting a discount (for example 20%) left the old value in one of the two places it is saved, so the quote and agreement still gave 20% off. Typing a negative number had the same effect. Both places are now always updated together (`src/components/ConfigurationForm.tsx`, `tests/unit/manageSaasPricingUI.test.tsx`).
  - Discounts are now capped at 100% (main and custom-items discount). Above 100%, the quote total went negative.
  - All discount math goes through one helper, `src/utils/discount.ts` (`tests/unit/discount.test.ts`). The discount amount is rounded to cents before it is subtracted, so the discount line and the final total always add back up to the total.
  - The Deal Desk and agreement emails now show the same discount % and amount as the quote page.
  - Removed an old Quote page message about a "$2,500 minimum / 10% cap" that no longer exists.
- **Multi combination: the custom plan no longer passes on an already-discounted total.** "Proceed with Custom Plan Selection" handed the quote a total with the discount already taken off, while every other plan passes on the full price. The emails then showed the discounted amount as the "Subtotal" (e.g. Subtotal $8,000, Discount −$2,000, Final $8,000), and the agreement could take the discount off a second time. The custom plan now passes on the full price. The email Subtotal lines and the preview `{{total price}}` use the same total that the discount is taken from (`src/components/PricingComparison.tsx`, `src/components/QuoteGenerator.tsx`, `tests/unit/pricingPerCombinationPlans.test.tsx`).
- **Saved quotes: "Total Cost" now shows the price after discount.** The saved-quote views (quote preview, quote email, Quote Information panel) and the older approval page used the total before discount. With 20% off a $10,000 quote they showed $10,000 instead of $8,000. They now show $8,000. Where the costs are listed line by line, a "Discount (20%): −$2,000" line is added so the lines still add up. The downloaded PDF quote's document card also saves the discounted total. All of them use `getQuoteTotals` in `src/utils/discount.ts` (`src/components/QuoteManager.tsx`, `src/components/ApprovalWorkflow.tsx`, `src/components/QuoteGenerator.tsx`).
- **Data Sprawl: one project configuration per selected exhibit.** On Manage → Data-Sprawl and MANAGE + Sprawl, selecting two Content Sprawl exhibits (e.g. DropBox and Egnyte) showed only one configuration, because groups were built per sprawl type from manual checkboxes. Each selected exhibit folder now gets its own card with its own users and quantity, its own tier rate and its own agreement row (e.g. "Data Sprawl – DropBox (345 GB)"). The Content/Message/Email checkboxes are gone; types come from the exhibit names, in "Content Sprawl Egnyte" or "Egnyte Content Sprawl" form, with Included and Not Included files merged into one card. The MANAGE + Sprawl licence prices on the total users across cards. A sprawl agreement with no exhibits selected can no longer be submitted. Quotes saved before this change price exactly as before (`src/utils/sprawlGroups.ts`, `src/utils/sprawlName.ts`, `src/hooks/useSprawlGroups.ts`, `src/components/SprawlGroupCard.tsx`, `src/utils/pricing.ts`, `src/utils/sprawlAgreement.ts`).
  - Content is rounded to whole dollars per card, so very small amounts split across cards can round differently than a single combined line.
  - Also fixed: the pricing comparison view no longer breaks when two lines share a sprawl type.
  - Also fixed: returning to Configure after "Select Data Sprawl" no longer ticks every sprawl exhibit; only the hand-picked ones stay selected. Folder and card names drop the plan wording ("Standard Plan - Standard").
- **Manage agreements now include their exhibits.** Exhibits attached to a Manage template (Data-Sprawl, mange+sprawl) were ticked on the Configure page but never added to the generated or emailed agreement. Every ticked exhibit attached to the chosen template is now merged, whatever the plan (Manage has one flat price). Files with Include / Not Include use today's grouped headings; otherwise they are appended whole, Include first, then untyped, then Not Include. A file that fails to load is reported instead of dropped silently (`src/utils/manageExhibitMerge.ts`, `keepAllContent` option in `src/utils/docxMerger.ts`).
- **Configure page: exhibits from a previous type no longer end up in the agreement.** After going Combination → Overage or Manage → back to Combination, the exhibit picker showed nothing ticked, but the generated agreement still contained the earlier Combination or Data-Sprawl exhibits from hidden per-pair rows. Clicking a plan tab, choosing Combination or Overage, or choosing a Manage template now clears both the ticked exhibits and those rows, so the document matches the screen (`src/components/ConfigurationForm.tsx`, `tests/unit/exhibitTypeSwitchClear.test.tsx`).
- **Configure page: switching plans no longer keeps the previous agreement template.** Generating a Multi combination agreement, switching to Manage, then back to Migrate and choosing Overage used a stale template until the page was refreshed. Clicking a plan tab now drops the old template, and a new one is picked only once a combination or agreement is chosen, so Overage loads its own template straight away (`src/utils/templateSelection.ts`, `tests/unit/templateSelection.test.ts`).
- **Approval workflow: stale approval pages can no longer overturn a decision.** Technical and Legal approvers share one group-mailbox link, so a teammate's already-open page could still Approve or Deny after someone else had acted. `PUT /api/approval-workflows/:id/step/:stepNumber` now refuses these clicks with HTTP 409, and the Team Lead, Technical and Legal pages show the reason (for example "This document is already approved by the Technical Team.") and reload the real status.
  - Opening an approval email link after that step is already decided now opens the document read-only, with a banner and footer saying who approved or denied it and when. Before, the page landed on an empty dashboard. Each link is handled once, so the document no longer reopens after an action and the preview is not fetched twice.
  - Steps must be decided in order. A closed step, or a step on an approved or denied deal, cannot be changed, except that Legal can still mark the Deal Desk step "Notified" after approval. A Deal Desk "deny" cannot reopen an approved deal.
  - When two people click at the same moment, exactly one click wins and the other gets the 409 message. Only the target step is written, so a teammate's comment is no longer lost.
  - The request body accepts only `status` (`approved` or `denied`) and `comments` (text, at most 10,000 characters). Empty requests, other statuses and malformed step numbers are rejected. The old `/step/99` and `/step/abc` paths that approved a whole deal now return 404.
  - The creator's denial email is sent only after the denial is saved.
  - Rules live in `approval-step-guard.cjs` and are covered by `tests/unit/approvalStepGuard.test.ts`, `approvalWorkflowStepConflict.test.ts` and `approvalStaleAction.test.tsx`.
  - Known and still open, planned for a later round: the approval routes still have no server-side authentication.
